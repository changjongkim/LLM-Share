% Design: publish and attach as operations on mappings, by the entries of the
% two page tables and the pages they refer to. The pages of the prefix are
% private to the publisher while it computes them, immutable once it has
% published them (one protection change per tensor, after the device has
% completed its writes), and shared once an agent maps them read-only and
% reads them from the CPU, which sets the accessed flags. What a process
% writes afterwards lies in pages of its own.
